The Academy of Raya Lucaria sits in the middle of the great lake of Liurnia, and is the place of study for Glintstone Sorcerers. Long ago, Rennala charmed the Academy with her lunar magic. She became the Academy's master and established the house of Caria as royalty. However, when Rennala's husband Radagon left her to become the second Elden Lord, Rennala's heart went along with him. Those at the Academy then realised that Rennala was no champion, and she was confined to the great library. She still clutches the amber egg given to her by Radagon, which contains the Great Rune of the Unborn.
The Academy offers multiple fields of study, known as Conspectuses. Scholars of note are permitted to don Glintstone Crowns modelled after notable sorcerers in their chosen fields of study. Azur and Lusat, regarded as founding glintstone sorcerers, founded the Karolos and Olivinus Conspectuses, respectively. Most of the sorcerers found within wear Glintstone Crowns that denote their area of study, and hint at the types of sorceries they may wield.
The Academy declared that it would not take part in The Shattering, and repelling seals were cast upon its doors, blocking outside access to anyone not in possession of a Academy Glintstone Key. Such a key may be found on an island west of the Academy, guarded by Glintstone Dragon Smarag.

### [4] Reduvia

Its skill, Reduvia Blood Blade, is essentially a version of the Blood Blade skill that costs twice as much FP and deals a little less Stance damage in exchange for not costing any HP to cast and having 50% more base Blood Loss (90 instead of 60).
Since this skill hits with both the projectile and the blade, it deals much more damage and inflicts more Blood Loss build-up when used within the dagger's range.
Ultimately, Blood Blade will do about 4% more damage at half cost with equivalent Arcane and an Occult affinity, making it the stronger alternative if the user is willing to stomach the self-damage and the relatively lower Blood Loss isn't a problem.
The Blood Loss build-up scales with Arcane.
Not counting the Wakizashi this is the strongest dagger for a hybrid Dexterity/Arcane build and the second strongest dagger for a hybrid Strength/Arcane or pure Arcane build, after the Bloodstained Dagger.
However, the Bloodstained's advantage is rather slight, only 8 AP (Attack Power) for the Str/Arc build and a mere 1 AP for the pure Arc build and the Reduvia will consistently have 10 more points of Blood Loss build-up.
The Bandit starting Origin has enough stats to wield this weapon without any level ups or stat-boosting equipment and items.

**Hỏi:** Flame, Cleanse Me giúp xử lý poison và scarlet rot như thế nào?

Flame, Cleanse Me là incantation của Fire Monks: niệm lên một ngọn lửa bên trong thiêu rụi độc tố, vừa làm giảm lượng tích tụ poison và scarlet rot, vừa chữa khỏi hẳn hai trạng thái đó nếu đã bị dính [2][3]. Đổi lại, phép này để lại vết bỏng nhẹ trên người niệm — đúng như mô tả "subtle burns" [2].

### [2] Bloodhound's Fang

https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Bloodhound%27s_Fang#Notes

The Unique Skill: Bloodhound's Finesse is an extremely strong two-part attack that also has some defensive use as it dashes backwards with the initial attack and then has Invincibility Frames with the follow-up attack, though it does have the downside of being somewhat hard to control and being parryable.
Bloodhound's Fang is one of only two Somber Smithing Stone-based weapons that can receive Weapon Buffs.
Bloodflame Blade is generally the strongest option for this, as the combination of extra Fire Attack Power and additional Blood Loss build-up is quite good for a fairly small investment in Faith and Arcane required to cast it.
As implied in the description, the jump attacks of Bloodhound's Fang deal much more damage when compared to the jump attacks of the same weapon class. This is due to the jump attacks of the Bloodhound's Fang having higher motion values than usual.
The motion values for all four jump attacks on the Bloodhound's Fang are 118% (one-handed jumping light attacks), 142% (one-handed jumping heavy attacks), 128% (two-handed jumping light attacks) and 151% (two-handed jumping heavy attacks). The typical motion values on Curved Greatswords for these same attacks are 107%, 127%, 112% and 132%.
Curiously, dual-wield jumping attacks with the Bloodhound's Fang do not receive this boost.
As the scaling letters imply, this weapon scales the best with Dexterity though an even split of Strength and Dexterity gives almost equal results, especially when two-handed.

The Raya Lucaria Grand Library is one of the highest points in the Academy of Raya Lucaria located in Liurnia of the Lakes. It is where Queen Rennala was sequestered following her abandonment by Radagon and the subsequent civil war between the sorcerers of the academy.
Here, Rennala cradles the Great Rune of The Unborn nestled in an amber egg, the farewell present from Radagon, and attempts to perfect her art of rebirth. She is surrounded by many imperfect creations, dubbed her "sweetings."
Sellen and Witch-Hunter Jerren will have their final confrontation here, depending on who the Tarnished sides with, either Jerren will remain outside or Sellen will take her place in the inside where she may continue to sell the Tarnished any glintstone sorceries.
There is a single chest in the library, which is locked by the Discarded Palace Key. Within is the Dark Moon Ring needed to complete Ranni's questline and unlock the Cathedral of Manus Celes.

**Đáp án thật:**

Radagon's Soreseal raises Vigor, Endurance, Strength, and Dexterity by 5 each [1]. The tradeoff is that you take more damage — the listed penalty is +15% damage taken, i.e. lower damage negation [1]. Because of that, it tends to shine on lower-level characters and becomes riskier as enemies hit harder later on, so it's often worth swapping out once your Vigor gets near the first softcap [4].

### [4] Radagon's Soreseal

https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Radagon%27s_Soreseal#Notes

Radagon's Soreseal fares best on lower level builds, but can become a bit of a trap if worn too long. In the early game, the stat increases more than make up for the extra damage taken in practice due to the high returns of HP at lower levels of Vigor, the significant bump in flat Defense from effectively being 20 levels higher, and the increased Equipment Load allowing players to wear heavier armor sooner. The increased Strength and Dexterity also have greater utility early on, when players have fewer points to spare to meet weapon requirements. However, as the player levels up their stats, they will eventually reach a point of diminishing returns, where +5 to a given stat simply doesn't provide as large an impact proportionally. Meanwhile, -15% Damage Negation only becomes an increasingly dire debuff as the player gets further into the game and enemies hit for larger and larger amounts of damage.
Once the player hits Vigor's first softcap of 40, it's better to remove the Soreseal and replace it with another talisman that provides either a percentage increase to the associated attributes or a flat stat increase without lowering Damage Negation. For example, more HP can be obtained from the Crimson Amber Medallions, more Equipment Load from the Arsenal Charms, a mixture of both boons from Erdtree's Favors, and more Strength or Dexterity from their associated Heirlooms.
Considered a variant of Radagon's Scarseal, the two cannot be equipped at the same time.
This is one of the eight talismans needed for the Legendary Talismans trophy/achievement.
